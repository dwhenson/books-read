This book was fantastic on so many levels. Three generations of Lakeland farming described in an unromantic, clear-eyed perspective, taking in the challenges and rewards, of a hard working life. I read this as part of my efforts to better understand the relationship between farmers and their land, and how conservation and rewilding is perceived. As much as just reading a book can,  this has helped me gain a glimpse of the depth of the relationship between farmers and their farm, and their livelihood and identity, and the animals, both domestic and wild that are part of that life. The writing is wonderful, and I was absolutely engaged, amazed and involved from start to finish.
