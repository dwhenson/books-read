I absolutely loved this book. It was dealing with a subject of no interest to me, about a character that I would have thought I couldn't care less about, and yet, I was hooked from about 5 pages in. This is writing at its best and literature doing what it does when it's at its best: placing me in a world that I knew nothing about, and barely new even existed, and making me feel thoroughly invested in its world, story and characters. Amazing.
