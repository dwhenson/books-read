A strong return to form from the author after the very poor [The Seventh Floor]({{< relref "/books/20250812-the-seventh-floor.md">}}), and possibly his best yet. The book's structure was unusual but worked well, and I was pleased to have a break from the usual cast of characters that have reoccurred in his books to date. I enjoyed this and it made for great reading on a couple of long train journeys and served well to take my mind of some heavy things going on at home. Recommended if you want a decent page turner.
