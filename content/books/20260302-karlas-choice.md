The author really capture the John le Carre's voice and writing style. And as happens to me so often with John le Carre's the story unfolded without me really noticing, and before we knew it we were knee deep in twists, and, to be honest, I should have paid more attention! So this is going to require another read from me in due course. But this was a great read, and a worthy addition to the George Smiley cannon.
