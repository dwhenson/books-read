I was stood next to the counter in Oxfam buying a book for my son when someone donated this book. I was intrigued that the book seller and customer both raved about the book, so I thought I'd give it a try. I have to say it was a wonderful surprise. Fantastic writing, and a lovely concept which if you were willing to go along with was really enjoyable and thought provoking. I loved the way it delved into the power of language and the nuance of translation. I found it a little one sided and would have liked some more depth in the counter argument, but maybe I am just biased? A wonderful, really enjoyable read.
