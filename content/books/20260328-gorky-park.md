I seem to be falling into the trap of just reading thrillers again! But this was a good one. Recommended by George Osbourne on Political Currency I thought I'd give it a go. I really enjoyed the pace of the book, as it took it's time to unravel and bring the characters together. It also captured in time the period it was written in really well, evoking a lovely sense of both Moscow and New York. An enjoyable and fun read.
