This was a good read. The author did a great job introducing the characters and limiting the number of characters that we were really involved with to number that could easily be coped with. I’ve watched a movie of the incident so I was familiar with a little bit of what went on, but the book provided really nice steps on the motivation of the people carrying out the attack the feelings and mentality of some hostages and their relationships between these two groups.
