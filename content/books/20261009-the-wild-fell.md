Another book that is part of my education in UK wildlife conservation. I was hoping to read this as a contrast to English Pastoral, but it just doesn't compare; in content or prose. I found some parts interesting, but I just struggled to get behind the author's journey from naïve newbie to less naïve not-so-newbie, and I found much of the content pretty bland. I probably learnt more than I realise, and perhaps it would have been better to read rather than listen to the author narrate the book.
