An unbelievable book detailing the hardship and trials that this group of men went through while maintaining a seemingly positive outlook. It is just incredible what they went through. I didn't know whether the men survived this ordeal and this added an extra level of investment for me. The passage of time, the patience the men needed is just unbelievable and unthinkable in today’s modern world. A real lesson in the power of waiting.
