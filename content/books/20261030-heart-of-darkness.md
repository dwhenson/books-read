It is hard to know what to make of this book really. In a way it was more a feeling than a story but the feeling it created was well done in the sense of claustrophobia and lawlessness was really well done. It seemed to book about the nature of humans and left their own devices some of the descriptions were amazing. I quite enjoyed it, but I think having been to the Congo and the forest and having some inkling of what it's like might have made it more accessible to me.
