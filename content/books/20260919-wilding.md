This was recommended as a good starting point to begin learning about rewilding and conservation in the UK context (I've just started a new job in this area). It's a well written and really interesting account of how the landscape of one farm has changed. I'd love to see the place myself to see if the lyrical descriptions in the book match the reality on the ground. But, even allowing for a little artistic licence, it sounds amazing, and just great to hear a first hand account of the resilience of nature. Inspiring.
