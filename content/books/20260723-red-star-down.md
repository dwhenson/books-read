I liked the idea of interspersing real events elaborating stories around them, and this made for enjoyable reading. It gave me that quick thrill that you get when you see a place you know in a movie or on the television, but the story and the characters just weren't enough for me. I just didn't care enough about them or find them that engaging. The two strands of the story did some together by the end, but it just felt forced and contrived, and too long, to me.
