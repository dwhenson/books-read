I was motivated to reread this after hearing a discussion about it on a podcast. I hadn't read it since I was a student. It is so good, and has possibly one of the best starts to a novel ever. Within a few paragraphs it feels like you are right there, and the matter of fact descriptions of things that are almost the same as they are in our world, but at the same time completely different are just wonderful, and scary. I didn't really get on board with some of the later parts of the book, in particular the effort put into 'correcting' Winston, but this is a very minor quibble, and is probably needed for the book to make it's point.
